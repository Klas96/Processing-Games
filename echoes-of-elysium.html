Echoes of Elysium

About the Game

Echoes of Elysium is a top-down cyberpunk space RPG. Explore two maps, talk to mysterious characters with voiced dialogue, collect Aetherian Fragments, and battle the UEC Sentinel boss — all set to an atmospheric soundtrack.

✨ Features

  • Story & NPCs: 5 characters with unique voiced dialogue via ElevenLabs TTS
  • Shooting: Tap to fire energy bolts at enemies
  • Fragment Collection: Find 3 Aetherian Fragments to unlock the portal on Map 1
  • Boss Fight: Face the UEC Sentinel on Map 2 — enters rage mode at low HP
  • Health Pickups: Green orbs scattered across both maps
  • Objective Tracker: On-screen HUD shows your current goal
  • Atmospheric Audio: Background music + SFX for footsteps, shooting, and portals

🎮 How to Play

  1. Use the on-screen joystick to move your character
  2. Walk up to NPCs and press Talk to hear their story
  3. Collect the 3 glowing purple Aetherian Fragments on Map 1
  4. Step through the portal to reach Map 2
  5. Tap anywhere on screen to shoot energy bolts at the UEC Sentinel
  6. Defeat the Sentinel, then reach the portal to win
